USB CONNECTION

Plug in.

Insert your microSD card and connect your Flipper Zero with a USB data cable. Close qFlipper so the web installer can use the port.

FIRMWARE UPDATE

Load Eddy.

Open the Eddy updater, connect your device, and install the main build. Leave your Flipper plugged in through the restart.

INTERACTIVE PREVIEW

Meet Eddy.

Play around with the Flipper Zero emulator to see Eddy in action! Start by pressing the center or down button.

Using the Flipper buttons
Open Now Playing
Press Right from your library. Press Back to pick up where you left off.
Save a playlist shortcut
Highlight Liked Songs or a playlist and hold an unused arrow to save it. Hold that arrow again to start the playlist. To replace a shortcut, highlight the new playlist, hold OK, then choose its arrow.
Queue a song
Open a playlist or Liked Songs, highlight the track you want to hear later, and press OK → Add to queue. Your current song keeps playing.
Save the current song
In Now Playing, press Down, choose a playlist, and press OK.
Control playback
In Now Playing, press OK to pause or play. Left restarts the song or goes back a track. Right skips to the next track.
