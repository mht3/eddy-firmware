Spotify setup

Currently, I’m testing Spotify over Bluetooth on the Flipper Zero with my own Spotify developer key. I originally hoped everyone could use their own key. Spotify allows one Client ID per integration, so personal library access is still in private testing. I realize this is not ideal, and I’m working on it. For now, I hope this guide makes the setup easier to understand.

Connecting to Spotify

If you scanned the QR code on your Flipper, you’re in the right place. You can try the preview below without signing in or creating a developer account.

My prototype uses a computer to connect the Flipper to Spotify. The setup starts with Spotify selected under Extras in the firmware installer. After the Flipper restarts, I open Apps → Spotify. The companion on my computer handles Spotify sign-in and Bluetooth pairing. Playback stays on the active Spotify device, which can be a phone.

Using the Flipper buttons

Open Now Playing
Press Right from your library. Press Back to pick up where you left off.
Save a playlist shortcut
In Playlists, highlight a playlist and hold an unused arrow to save it. Hold that arrow again to start the playlist. To replace a shortcut, highlight the new playlist, hold OK, then choose its arrow.
Queue, save, or change volume
In Now Playing, press Down to reach the buttons. Use Left or Right to choose a button, then press OK. The first two add the current song to your queue or a playlist. The volume button opens a slider you adjust with Up and Down.

Try the Spotify preview

Open Morning Swim, queue a track, or press Up in Now Playing to follow the timed lyrics I’ve included for the sample songs.
