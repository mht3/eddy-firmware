EDDY WIKI

Project origins.

Eddy Firmware began as a fork of Momentum Firmware for Flipper Zero. We started from the source code published by Next-Flip and are building an independent interface inspired by ocean eddies. Our wave logo represents the current. On the home screen, Eddy surfaces in a whirlpool. We adapted the smiling dolphin from the original firmware artwork included in Momentum.

Thank you to Momentum’s maintainers and contributors, and to Flipper Devices for the original firmware. Eddy retains the upstream license and contributor notices.
