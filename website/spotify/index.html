Spotify setup

Use your own Spotify Developer account to register Eddy and save your Client ID to your Flipper during setup. The installer prepares your update in your browser. I never receive your ID.

Your Flipper uses its built-in Bluetooth radio. For live playlists, use a browser with Web Bluetooth, such as Chrome on Android. iPhone Safari can complete Spotify sign-in, but it can’t send playlists to the Flipper. Have an idea? Leave me a suggestion on GitHub.

Create your Spotify developer app

  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard. Sign in with your Spotify Premium account. If this is your first app, you’ll see “You haven’t created any apps yet.” Look for the purple Create app button in the upper right. Open the dashboard.
  2. Click Create app. Spotify opens the blank form shown below. Scroll down to see the API choices and the Save button.
  3. Fill out the form. Use these values, working from top to bottom.
    App name
    Eddy
    App description
    A Spotify interface for the Flipper Zero.
    Website
    https://mht3.github.io/eddy-firmware
    Redirect URIs
    Paste https://mht3.github.io/eddy-firmware/spotify/, including the final slash. Click Add and check that the address appears in the list.
    Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?
    Check Web API only.
  4. Check your completed form. Read Spotify’s Developer Terms of Service and Design Guidelines. If you accept them, check the agreement box. Click Save at the bottom left.
  5. Copy your Client ID. On the Eddy app page, open Settings. Click the copy icon beside Client ID. You can leave the Client Secret hidden.
  6. Paste the ID into the Eddy installer. Select Spotify under Extras. Under Your build, paste the ID into Spotify Client ID, then install or download your update. Open the installer.

Connect your Flipper

  1. After the update, open Apps → Spotify on your Flipper. Scan the code on its screen with your phone and open the link in your browser.
  2. Spotify opens automatically. Check the app name and requested permissions, then approve access. Spotify returns you to the connection page.
  3. With Bluetooth on and the Spotify app still open on the Flipper, select Choose Flipper in the browser and pick your device. Start a song in Spotify and leave the browser tab open while using the Flipper.
