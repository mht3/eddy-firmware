Bluetooth pairing.

Pair your phone with your Flipper once. Spotify uses that same connection.

On your Flipper

  1. From the home screen, press Up. Move to the Settings gear and press OK.
  2. Open Bluetooth. Set it to ON and press Back to apply.

On your phone

  1. On iPhone, open Settings → Bluetooth. On Android, touch and hold Bluetooth in Quick Settings, then tap Pair new device.
  2. Tap your Flipper’s name in the device list. Follow the pairing prompt on your phone and Flipper.

Already paired? Keep Bluetooth on and your Flipper nearby. The devices remember each other when you power the Flipper back on.

My Flipper isn’t connecting

In your phone’s Bluetooth settings, tap the saved Flipper to reconnect. If you previously paired only with “Eddy Music,” pair your Flipper under its normal device name once.
