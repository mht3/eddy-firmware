Spotify setup

Spotify works over Bluetooth without any additional Flipper Zero modules. To give your Flipper Zero access to Spotify while testing your own developer application, follow the instructions below. You’ll need Spotify Premium and Eddy on your iPhone. Check iPhone beta availability.

I realize this is not ideal. Spotify’s current guidelines limit who can use a development app. Regardless, I hope this Spotify setup is painless for you and know that I’m currently working on a better fix. Feel free to leave a GitHub issue for suggestions!

Create your Spotify developer app

  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard. Sign in with your Spotify Premium account. If this is your first app, you’ll see “You haven’t created any apps yet.” Look for the purple Create app button in the upper right. Open the dashboard.
  2. Click Create app. Spotify opens the blank form shown below. Scroll down to see the API choices and the Save button.
  3. Fill out the form. Use these values, working from top to bottom.
    App name
    Eddy
    App description
    A Spotify interface for the Flipper Zero.
    Website
    https://mht3.github.io/eddy-firmware
    Redirect URIs
    Paste https://mht3.github.io/eddy-firmware/spotify/, including the final slash. Click Add and check that the address appears in the list.
    Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?
    Select Web API. You’ll add the iOS settings after saving the form.
  4. Check your completed form. Read Spotify’s Developer Terms of Service and Design Guidelines. If you accept them, check the agreement box. Click Save at the bottom left.
  5. Enable sign-in on iPhone. Open Settings → Edit for your Eddy developer app. Select iOS alongside Web API. Add the bundle ID dev.eddyfirmware.spotify and the second redirect URI eddyspotify://spotify-login. Keep the HTTPS redirect you entered above and click Save.
  6. Copy your Client ID. Open your Eddy app in the Spotify dashboard and go to Settings → Basic Information. Click the two overlapping squares at the right end of the Client ID field, circled below. Keep the ID handy for the iPhone step. Leave the Client Secret hidden.

Install Spotify on your Flipper

  1. Plug the Flipper into your computer with a USB data cable. In the Eddy installer, include Spotify under Extras.
  2. Select Connect Flipper Zero, choose your device, and follow the installer until the Flipper restarts.
  3. On the Flipper, open Apps → Spotify. Scan its QR code with your iPhone to open Eddy or see the companion installation instructions.

Connect with Eddy on your iPhone

  1. Keep Spotify open on the Flipper. In the Eddy iPhone app, tap Connect device, choose your Flipper’s name, and accept the Bluetooth pairing prompt if it appears.
  2. On Log in to Spotify, paste the Client ID you copied into Spotify Client ID. Tap Log in to Spotify, approve access in Spotify, and return to Eddy. If you’ve finished onboarding, you can find sign-in under Devices → Spotify.
  3. Follow the prompts on the Flipper. Once your library appears, open Playlists to pick a song. If Spotify asks for an active player, start a song in Spotify on your iPhone and try again.
