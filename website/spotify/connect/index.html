    How Eddy uses this connection

    This tab keeps the Spotify session and sends your commands to Spotify. While connected, it sends track and playlist information to your paired Flipper. Your four playlist shortcuts stay in this browser. Choose Unlink Spotify to clear this session, or remove Eddy’s access in Spotify.
