Your playlists on Flipper Zero.

Eddy on your iPhone connects to Spotify. Use the buttons on your Flipper to queue a track or start a favorite playlist.

Set up Eddy for iPhone

Eddy for iPhone

Already installed? Open Eddy. The Demo tab lets you try sample playlists offline before you connect your Flipper.

First, download TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone.

View the Eddy beta on TestFlight

When the beta is accepting testers, open the invitation on the same iPhone and tap Accept, then Install.

Requires iOS 17 or later. Android isn’t supported yet.

Connect your library

On your Flipper
Open Apps → Spotify and leave it open. Scan the QR code with your iPhone to open Eddy or find its installation instructions.
In Eddy on your iPhone
Tap Connect device and choose your Flipper. Enter your Client ID, tap Log in to Spotify, and approve access.
Back on your Flipper
Open Playlists, choose a playlist and press OK on Browse tracks. Press Right from your library for Now Playing.
