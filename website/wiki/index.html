EDDY WIKI

Project origins.

I’m a PhD student in computer science at UC San Diego. Eddy is my take on the Flipper Zero. I named it after the little currents that turn back on themselves in the ocean. The swirl in the logo is one seen from above, and Eddy is the dolphin who swims through it on the home screen.

I started by forking Momentum Firmware, the open-source project from Next-Flip. I built the ocean-themed desktop on that foundation and adapted the smiling dolphin from the original Flipper artwork. I’m grateful to the Momentum contributors and to Flipper Devices for making their work available.

If you enjoy using my Flipper Zero firmware, you can support me with a donation on Ko-fi. If something goes wrong, report a bug on GitHub. I’d also love to hear your suggestions.
